## Preliminary identification
Amoxicillin is the suspected medicine because the event was reported after treatment began. This chronology is a reason to investigate, not proof that the medicine caused the rash.

## Causality and seriousness limitations
Causality cannot be concluded from this brief scenario. Alternative explanations, previous exposure, dechallenge/rechallenge information, and clinical assessment are missing. Seriousness cannot be formally assessed from the limited facts. Missing details must not be interpreted as proof that serious features are absent.

## Follow-up questions

## Conclusion
This fictional case illustrates how to identify a suspected medicine using the reported timeline, document the event, avoid overstating causality, and list information needed for follow-up. It is not a validated regulatory submission.
