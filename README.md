# Task 1 — ADR Identification Basics
**Pharmacovigilance learning project | Fictional educational case**

> **Important:** This is a fictional educational scenario only. It is not a real patient report, medical advice, or a validated regulatory submission.

## Purpose
Demonstrate structured review of a suspected adverse drug reaction (ADR): patient/reaction details, medication history, chronology, preliminary assessment, and missing information for follow-up.

## Case summary
A fictional 34-year-old adult is prescribed amoxicillin for a presumed bacterial throat infection. On day 3 of treatment, an itchy red rash on the trunk and arms is reported. The limited scenario does not report breathing difficulty or facial swelling. Exact dose, allergy history, concomitant medicines, clinical findings, and outcome are unknown.

## Preliminary assessment
- **Suspected medicine:** Amoxicillin, based on the reported timeline only.
- **Reported event:** Itchy red rash on the trunk and arms.
- **Timeline:** Treatment starts on day 1; rash is reported on day 3.
- **Causality:** Cannot be established from the limited information. A temporal relationship does not prove causality.
- **Seriousness:** Cannot be formally determined from this incomplete scenario.
- **Outcome:** Not provided.

## Follow-up information needed
1. Exact dose, route, start date and last dose.
2. Exact rash onset, description, progression, and other symptoms.
3. Relevant medical history and known allergies.
4. All concomitant medicines and recent infections/exposures.
5. Clinical evaluation, management, investigations, and outcome.
6. Whether the medicine was stopped and what happened afterward.
